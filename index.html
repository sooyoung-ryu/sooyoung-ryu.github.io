Sooyoung Ryu

I am a senior undergraduate at Seoul National University, currently working remotely with Prof. Chelsea Finn at Stanford University. Previously, as an exchange student at EPFL, I worked with Prof. Mathieu Salzmann at EPFL CVLab.

My research focuses on robot learning, particularly memory, continual policy improvement, and world models. I aim to build general-purpose embodied agents that learn from experience, adapt to new environments, and acquire new skills while retaining prior knowledge.

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in Fall 2027. I would be happy to discuss research opportunities or potential collaborations—please get in touch.
